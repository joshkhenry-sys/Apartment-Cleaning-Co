FAQ

Questions? We've got you.

What is move-out cleaning?

Move-out cleaning is a detailed cleaning service designed to prepare an apartment or home after you've moved your belongings out. It can help prepare the space for a landlord, inspection, sale, or next resident.

Do you clean apartments?

Yes. We clean apartments, condos, townhomes, and houses throughout the Denver metro area.

Do you offer move-in cleaning?

Yes. Move-in cleaning is available for people who want their new home cleaned before settling in.

Do you offer deep cleaning?

Yes. Deep cleaning is available for homes and apartments that need additional attention beyond a standard cleaning.

How much does cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on the size and condition of the space, the service requested, and the amount of cleaning required. Request a free quote and we'll discuss the details with you.
